How it works

Two independent reads, one honest verdict

The maths never has a football opinion. The analyst never sees enough to judge value. They meet only at the end — and where they agree is the strongest signal on the page.

De-vigged fair prices

Bookmaker odds bake in a margin. We strip it out of every book and average to a consensus fair probability, then line-shop the best price you can actually get.

The sharp-line check

Pinnacle and the exchanges run tiny margins and move first on real money. Every edge is cross-checked against that sharp line — the closest thing to a true probability.

A grounded analyst

An AI analyst researches each match — form, table, team news, head-to-head — and writes a read grounded only in what it found. It is forbidden from inventing stats.

Why trust it

Built to be honest, not to look impressive

Most tipster sites are engineered to sell confidence. This one is engineered to tell you the truth about the numbers — including the parts that make the edges look smaller.

Exchange commission is priced in

Exchanges show the best headline price on almost everything — but take a cut of your winnings. Edges here are measured on what you actually collect, so a 1% edge is a real 1%, not a mirage.

No fake predictions

These are +EV leans and pricing soft-spots, not score forecasts. Club league markets are highly efficient; edges are small and variance dominates. The app says so, everywhere.

You always know how old a price is

Odds are cached, and the app shows the age in plain sight — green when fresh, red when it has had time to move. Always confirm the price on your betslip before you stake.

Your record, kept honest

The My Bets journal snapshots the model's fair price and EV at the moment you log a bet — so over time you can check whether the edges were real, not just claimed.
